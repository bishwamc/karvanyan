# Release 4.1.1 – desktop road screen

- The "camel tape" (route strip drawing of overall distance) is gone from the road screen on every width. A one-line chapter title with a Map link stays.
- On wide screens the cartoon scene is now the main picture, top left, with the weather, today's leg and the progress bar under it. The margin card sits above it; pace, rations, party health and supplies are on the right.
- The scene is wider and shorter (5:2) so the whole picture shows above the control bar at 1366 x 768.
- Version label and `?v=` numbers are 4.1.1. Phone layout is unchanged.
